# Chart Generation - Sample Prompts

Sample user prompts you can use to ask the AI to generate chart visualizations based on the normalized columns in your sales dataset:

### 1. Sales Performance & Trends
*   **Monthly Sales Trend (Line/Bar Chart)**: 
    > "Create a line chart showing the monthly total Sales for 2019 to identify seasonal trends."
*   **Hourly Activity (Line Chart)**: 
    > "Plot the total number of orders by Hour of the day to find the peak times when customers place orders."
*   **Weekly Sales Volume (Bar Chart)**: 
    > "Generate a bar chart of total Sales grouped by Day of the week to see which days perform best."

### 2. Product Analysis
*   **Top Products by Quantity (Horizontal Bar Chart)**: 
    > "Create a horizontal bar chart showing the top 10 products by total Quantity Ordered, sorted from highest to lowest."
*   **Revenue Contribution by Product (Horizontal Bar Chart)**: 
    > "Plot the total Sales revenue generated by each unique Product."
*   **Price vs. Quantity Relationship (Scatter Plot)**: 
    > "Generate a scatter plot showing the average Price Each of products on the x-axis and the average Quantity Ordered on the y-axis to see if cheaper items sell in higher quantities."

### 3. Geographical/City Insights
*   **City Revenue Breakdown (Pie Chart)**: 
    > "Create a pie chart showing the percentage contribution of each City to the total Sales."
*   **Average Order Value by City (Bar Chart)**: 
    > "Plot a bar chart showing the average transaction value (Sales) for each City."

### 4. Advanced/Multi-Dimension Analysis
*   **Top Product Sales by City (Grouped Bar Chart)**: 
    > "Plot a grouped bar chart comparing the sales of the top 3 products ('USB-C Charging Cable', 'Lightning Charging Cable', 'AAA Batteries (4-pack)') across 'New York City', 'Los Angeles', and 'San Francisco'."
